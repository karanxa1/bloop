what bloop does

six jobs. one blob.

acts, not chats

bloop does not just answer. it books, files, sends, and fixes — taking real actions inside your real apps while you get on with your day.

proves every step

every action lands in a hash-chained verify trace, and every write is confirmed by read-back before bloop moves on. receipts, not vibes.

remembers you

preferences, people, past fixes — bloop keeps a persistent memory between sessions, so you never have to explain yourself twice.

any tool, one blob

plug in any mcp server from the marketplace — mail, calendar, docs, tickets, the weird internal tool — and the blob just knows what to do with it.

picks its brain

choose between frontier models and swap whenever you like. same blob, new brain — pick the thinker that fits the job.

runs code for real

when clicking is not enough, bloop writes and runs code in an isolated cloudflare sandbox. real execution, and nothing touches your machine.
